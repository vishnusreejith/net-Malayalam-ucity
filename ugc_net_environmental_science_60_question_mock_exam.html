High-Yield UGC NET Formula Sheet

1. Ecosystem Energetics

  • NPP: GPP − Ra  |  NEP: GPP − (Ra + Rh)
  • Assimilation (A): I − F  |  NPE: (P / A) × 100
  • Light/Dark Bottle GPP: (DOL − DOD) / Δt
  • Residence Time (τ): Reservoir Size / Outflow Flux

2. Population & Biodiversity

  • Logistic Growth: dN/dt = rN(1 − N/K); MSY = rK/4
  • Lincoln-Petersen: N = (M × C) / R
  • Shannon Index: H′ = −Σ pi ln(pi); J′ = H′/ln(S)
  • Species-Area: S = cAz  |  Ne: 4NmNf / (Nm + Nf)

3. Air Quality & Dispersion

  • Unit Conv. (25°C): μg/m³ = (ppm × M × 1000) / 24.45
  • Gaussian Ground Centerline: C = [Q / (πuσyσz)] exp(−H²/2σz²)
  • Box Model: C = Cb + (qs · L) / (u · H)
  • Wind Power Law: u2 = u1 (z2 / z1)p

4. Air Pollution Control

  • ESP (Deutsch-Anderson): η = 1 − exp(−wA / Q)
  • Settling Chamber: vt = Q / (W · L)
  • Series Efficiency: ηtotal = 1 − (1 − η1)(1 − η2)
  • Baghouse Area: A = Q / (A/C Ratio)
